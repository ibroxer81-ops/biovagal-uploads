Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Punsnup is built to collect as little as possible. There are no accounts, no tracking and no adverts.

What stays on your device

Your saved favourite puns and your daily free-pun count are stored in your own browser (local storage). They never leave your device, and clearing your browser data removes them.

Payments

If you buy unlimited access, payment is handled by Stripe, which receives the details you enter at checkout (such as your email and card). We don't see or store your card details. See Stripe's privacy policy.

Hosting and fonts

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and uses Google Fonts. Like any website, these services receive your IP address and basic browser information when the page loads. See GitHub's privacy statement and Google's privacy policy.

Cookies

Punsnup doesn't set advertising or analytics cookies.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask what personal data we hold about you and ask for it to be corrected or deleted. Contact us on YouTube at @Punsnup.

Changes

We'll update this page if anything changes. The date at the top shows the latest version.
