Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

The Biovagal app is built to collect as little as possible. There are no accounts, no tracking and no adverts.

What stays on your device

Your practice days, minutes and membership status are stored in your own browser (local storage). They never leave your device, and clearing your browser data removes them.

Payments

If you become a member, payment is handled by Stripe, which receives the details you enter at checkout (such as your email and card). We don't see or store your card details. See Stripe's privacy policy.

Hosting and fonts

The app is hosted on GitHub Pages and uses Google Fonts, which receive your IP address and basic browser information when the page loads. See GitHub's privacy statement and Google's privacy policy.

Cookies

The app doesn't set advertising or analytics cookies.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask what personal data we hold about you and ask for it to be corrected or deleted. Message us on @biovagal on YouTube.
